Photo & Media Gallery

Relive our favorite PTA moments! We keep this page curated with a rotating highlight reel and link out to complete albums hosted on services with robust sharing features.

Replace the placeholder images above with photos that you store in the repository or link from a CDN. Always ensure you have photo release permissions before posting.

Best Options for Hosting Albums

  • Google Photos: Create shareable albums, organize by school year, and embed highlight reels with simple iframe snippets.
  • SmugMug: Offers password-protected galleries, watermarking, and easy download controls for families.
  • Flickr: Affordable storage, tagging, and community moderation tools.
  • SharePoint / OneDrive: Ideal if the district already uses Microsoft 365 for staff accounts.

Embedding External Galleries

Many services provide embed codes you can paste directly into this page. For example, Google Photos offers a "Create Link" option with HTML you can drop into a <iframe>. Keep accessibility in mind by including descriptive alt text.

Share Your Photos

Send event photos to media@summitridgepta.org or upload them to our shared Google Drive folder. Our communications team reviews each submission before publishing.
