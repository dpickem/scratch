Support Summit Ridge PTA

Every dollar fuels teacher grants, student programs, and community-building events. Choose the contribution method that fits your family or business.

Online Giving

Fast, secure, and accessible from any device.

  • Stripe Checkout: Embed a donation button linking to a hosted payment page.
  • PayPal: Use the pre-built donation widget for one-time gifts.
  • Givebutter: Track donor activity and share progress bars.
Donate with Stripe Give via PayPal

Offline Gifts

  • Mail checks to: Summit Ridge PTA, 1234 Summit Ave, Springfield, ST 99999.
  • Employer matching: Provide your receipt to unlock corporate matching gifts.
  • Sponsor a program: Email fundraising@summitridgepta.org to review current opportunities.

For in-kind donations, coordinate with our fundraising chair to arrange pickup or drop off.

Sponsor Recognition

Bronze Partners

$250–$499

  • Logo on PTA website sponsor page.
  • Shout-out in monthly newsletter.

Silver Partners

$500–$999

  • All Bronze benefits.
  • Logo displayed at two major events.
  • Dedicated social media thank-you post.

Gold Partners

$1,000+

  • All Silver benefits.
  • Banner placement at events all year.
  • Invitation to speak at annual meeting.

Financial Transparency

We publish budget summaries and annual financial reports in our document library. Members can request detailed statements from the treasurer at any time.

Monthly treasurer reports and Form 990 filings are archived under Financial Documents.

Questions?

Email treasurer@summitridgepta.org for receipts, tax ID information, or confirmation letters for donations.
