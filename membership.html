Join the Summit Ridge PTA

Membership dues directly fund programs that benefit every student. Anyone who cares about Summit Ridge can join—parents, guardians, teachers, grandparents, alumni, and community partners.

Membership Benefits

  • Empower classroom grants and student enrichment experiences.
  • Receive our monthly newsletter with key updates and volunteer needs.
  • Vote on PTA priorities, budgets, and leadership.
  • Access exclusive member resources and discounts from state/national PTA.
  • Build relationships with families and staff across grade levels.

2024–2025 Dues

Annual membership: $20 per adult

Household bundle (up to 3 adults): $45

Faculty & staff: $10

Join or Renew Online

Prefer to pay offline? Download the membership form and return it with cash or check to the front office.
