Volunteer Checklist

  • Complete district-required background check (if applicable).
  • Review the PTA volunteer handbook in our document library.
  • Sign in at the school office and wear your visitor badge.
  • Log your hours so we can recognize and thank you.

Automatic Confirmations

Pair your signup tool with automation platforms for timely communication:

  • Zapier: Add volunteers to a Google Sheet or email list automatically.
  • IFTTT: Send Slack or text alerts to committee chairs when new slots fill.
  • Google Apps Script: Customize email confirmations from Google Forms submissions.
